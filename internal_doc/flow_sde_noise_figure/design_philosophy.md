# Measured Drift

Measured Drift treats a technical result as a field observation: sparse marks, exact spacing, and calibrated contrast carry the argument before any sentence explains it. Form is disciplined and almost clinical, with the main evidence held in a generous plot area and the mechanism reduced to a few precise visual tokens. The result should feel meticulously crafted, as if every tick, label, and gap was placed after careful inspection.

Color is an information system rather than decoration. A warm signal marks the single-step trajectory; a cool signal marks the every-step trajectory; a restrained green accent marks the theoretical crossover. The palette should remain quiet enough for scientific use while still showing master-level execution through painstaking contrast control and small, deliberate changes in weight.

Composition favors asymmetric clarity: empirical measurements dominate one side, while the mechanistic explanation acts as a compact instrument panel. Equations are not paragraphs; they are visual levers. The work should look like the product of deep expertise, labored over with care, where the empty space is as intentional as the plotted line.

Rhythm comes from repeated denoising steps, shown as a chain of small transitions. Single-step noise appears as one emphasized impulse; every-step noise appears as a sequence of impulses tempered by repeated correction. The final artifact should communicate through visual causality, not through long text.

Typography is sparse, thin, and functional. Labels exist only where they anchor interpretation. The final figure must look polished enough for a paper appendix or lab meeting slide, with painstaking attention to alignment, hierarchy, and readable scale.
